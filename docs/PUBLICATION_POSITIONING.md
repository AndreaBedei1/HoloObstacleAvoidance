# Publication Positioning

Post-novelty-audit assessment (2026-08-10). Ordering by fit, not acceptance
probability alone.

| Venue | Fit | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **ICRA / IROS** | Strong | The S0–S3 predictiveness + SRCC study is exactly the kind of rigorous sim-to-real evaluation these venues reward; 6-8 pages forces the right scope (headline C1 + estimator C2, planner as component). Closest competitors (Mari 2026 in Sensors, DUViN 2025 arXiv) are NOT at these venues — room at the top. Deadlines: ICRA ~Sep, IROS ~Mar — real-pool campaign timing decides which. |
| **IEEE RA-L** | Strong | Kadian 2020 (our methodological anchor) is RA-L; journal format + optional conference presentation; fits if the campaign finishes off-cycle. |
| **IEEE Journal of Oceanic Engineering** | Good | EROAS 2024 precedent; values real marine experiments; longer format allows the full error-budget/ground-truth methodology. Slower. |
| **Ocean Engineering / Applied Ocean Research** | Good | Bergantin 2024 and Li 2026 precedents; solid marine audience; less methodological prestige than RA-L/ICRA. |
| **OCEANS (MTS/IEEE)** | Fallback | Good for an early infrastructure/protocol paper if the main study slips; not for the headline claim. |
| **IFAC CAMS / MCMC** | Fallback | Alinei-Poiana 2024 precedent (CAMS); control-community framing would underplay the perception-calibration contribution. |
| MDPI (Sensors/JMSE) | Avoid for headline | Fast but weaker signal; several threat papers live there — publishing next to them undercuts the "more rigorous than prior art" positioning. |

## Requirements the venue choice imposes

- ICRA/IROS/RA-L reviewers will demand: honest DWA tuning, the
  calibrated-vs-heuristic estimator ablations, SRCC with enough method pairs,
  pre-registered validity criteria, and open code/data (our differentiator).
- Full-text verification of the two aggregator-verified threat papers
  (Li AOR 2026; Li JMSE 2024) before submission.

## Decision point

Choose ICRA vs RA-L vs IROS when the real-pool campaign date is known
(pool access + wet authorization are the binding constraints).
